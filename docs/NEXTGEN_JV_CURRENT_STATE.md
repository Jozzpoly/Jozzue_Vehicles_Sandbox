# Nextgen Jozz Vehicle — Current State

Date: 2026-09-04

This document is the current high-level technical/research state of the Nextgen Jozz Vehicle project. It is intentionally more concrete and more replaceable than `NEXTGEN_JV_PROJECT_SOUL.md`.

Live source, exact experiment refs, reproducible evidence and current Owner judgement override this summary when they disagree.

## 1. Executive state

Three bounded research programs now define the current evidence frontier:

- **E1 — construction-loop research: CLOSED; central H0 unresolved.**
- **V0 — drivable physical steering consequence: CLOSED with technical PASS and final Owner readability PASS for research-carrier reuse only.**
- **R1 — direct spatial steering construction consequence: CLOSED with bounded positive causal-construction evidence and explicit non-acceptance of the current authoring/product quality.**

No current experiment defines the final JV architecture.

R1 materially advanced the project frontier: the Owner directly authored independent spatial steering pickup geometry, drove a vehicle whose steering consequence remained owned by the physical linkage, perceived changed driving behavior, returned to BUILD and continued experimenting. This is the first Owner-backed evidence that direct spatial construction can participate in a real mechanical causal path to driving consequence inside the intended creative loop.

R1 also exposed a major human-readability failure: the initial editable pickup targets were effectively hidden in/behind the wheel geometry, so the Owner initially could not discover or acquire them. Automated drag execution had privileged access to target coordinates and therefore did not establish human discoverability. The overall authoring experience remains raw, incomplete and far from product quality.

The project should **not** polish the R1 steering carrier into a builder. The next high-value move is a carefully bounded donor-forensics phase before representative-problem selection #2, with special attention to visual-model / rig / physics correspondence in historical JV material.

## 5. R1 disposition

R1 replaced V0's preset-only steering-authoring boundary with independent authored LEFT/RIGHT steering-arm pickup local X/Z geometry.

The bounded technical implementation preserved the real physical causal path:

`authored pickup geometry → physical steering-arm anchor → fixed-length physical tie-rod → knuckle/wheel → contact → trajectory`

The physical mechanism remained authority; the analytical oracle remained observational.

Machine evidence at the final checkpoint included:

- exact BUILD → RUN/DRIVE → BUILD authored-state recovery;
- independent left/right authored geometry;
- a same-radius / different-direction falsifier showing that response depends on spatial pickup direction rather than only arm length;
- asymmetric one-side edits without hidden symmetrization;
- fixed physical tie-rod constraints during PLAY;
- permissive continuation across analytical-oracle limitations;
- pointer interruption/cancel recovery;
- signed-angle branch-cut hardening;
- final code check `42/42` plus build PASS;
- applicable prior rendered browser suite `12/12`.

### Owner result

The Owner's short recordings are evidence samples rather than exhaustive logs; the Owner continued testing after recording and tested both pickup sides.

The Owner reported and observed that moving the pickup points changes driving behavior. Repeated BUILD → DRIVE → BUILD experimentation was possible, including extreme/permissive geometry.

This gives R1 a **bounded positive causal-construction result**.

It does **not** give R1 product or architecture acceptance.

The Owner's overall judgement was that the experience remains raw, incomplete, imperfect and requires substantial further work.

### Confirmed discoverability confound

In the first hands-on attempt the baseline pickup handles were effectively hidden in/behind the wheel geometry. The Owner initially could not identify or acquire the targets and first hit one by chance through the wheel.

The browser automation had privileged access to exact projected target coordinates, so its ability to execute a drag did not establish that a human could discover the same target.

Portable validation lesson:

> **operation execution with privileged target knowledge is not evidence of human affordance discoverability.**

Future direct-manipulation validation should keep reachability, visibility, identity and discoverability as separate claims.

Do not polish R1 into a builder. Do not respond to the closure by adding generic gizmos, snap/grid, numeric systems, better primitive vehicle presentation or broader steering features merely because the bounded experiment worked.

## 6. What is now relatively strong

The following directions have repeated support and should be treated as strong product constraints unless later evidence contradicts them.

### Short creative round trip

The core product loop remains:

`build → run → observe → improve → get in and drive`

The loop may cross process/window boundaries if the round trip remains natural and short.

### Causal mechanics

When the product presents a mechanism as causing vehicle behavior, that mechanism should own the consequence rather than being decorative around a hidden handling mapping.

R1 strengthens this requirement with Owner-backed direct-construction evidence.

### Direct, permissive construction

Direct spatial construction remains promising. R1 demonstrated that independent spatial mechanical geometry can feed a real causal path, and extreme authored geometry can remain runnable instead of being silently clamped into presets.

This does not yet define a general interaction grammar.

### Adaptive components

Components that adapt length, thickness, proportions or bounded functional geometry remain a strong product direction. Their correct automatic-adaptation versus explicit-intent/lock semantics are still open and should be earned by representative construction tasks.

### Owner-readable consequence

Machine-measured difference is insufficient when the Owner cannot perceive or understand the result. R1 adds that even technically correct direct manipulation is insufficient when the editable target is not discoverable.
