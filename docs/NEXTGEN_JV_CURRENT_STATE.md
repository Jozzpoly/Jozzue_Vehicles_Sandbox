## 5. Root methodological correction

Rep3 taught: a prototype can be too sparse to earn an experiential verdict.

Rep4 adds a second lesson: a technically rigorous integrated prototype can still spend too much effort hardening an **unproven interaction family**.

A large amount of work occurred before the Rep4 Owner gate: Stage A qualification, robustness work, visual correspondence, direct-interaction qualification and final BUILD→PLAY integration. That evidence is valuable, but the Owner still rejected the underlying experience.

Therefore retain this new rule:

> **Do not spend integration-grade validation on an interaction idea before it has earned enough Owner value to justify hardening.**

This does not reduce rigor. It moves rigor to the stage where it buys information.

## 6. Development method v2 — three prototype roles

Use three explicitly different lanes.

### A. Causal / implementation lab

Purpose: answer real mechanics/runtime/authority unknowns.

Characteristics:

- narrow scope;
- strong source/native/metamorphic validation;
- disposable when possible;
- no Owner feel request unless the apparatus actually manifests that quality.

Rep4 Stage A belongs here and is useful donor evidence.

### B. Interaction / experience spikes

Purpose: explore construction grammar, spatial interaction, recognition and creative breadth quickly.

Characteristics:

- several competing alternatives are preferred over polishing one local optimum;
- lightweight source/build/browser smoke before Owner use;
- may use kinematic or simulated behavior when clearly labelled and when no physical-causality claim is being made;
- must prioritize rapid, incremental, reversible direct manipulation;
- should use real donor visuals where they materially affect recognition;
- should intentionally test **wide walls**: multiple possible constructions, not one pre-authored topology with more editable coordinates.

Owner comparison happens early.

### C. Integration specimens

Purpose: connect a promising interaction grammar to qualified causal mechanics.

Only enter this lane after:

- the interaction idea earns positive or at least genuinely informative Owner evidence;
- the required causal seam is either already qualified or is worth a targeted implementation-lab probe.

Then use full causal/browser validation and stop again for Owner judgement.

## 7. Current frontier — construction grammar, not another suspension microbench

The next question is:

> **What direct construction grammar begins to give the Owner real structural agency rather than only hardpoint tuning?**

The next program should therefore be a **small comparative set of construction-grammar spikes**.

Minimum target is not `more editable points`. The Owner must be able to create a configuration whose meaningful relation/topology was not already completely encoded by the apparatus.

Leading interaction concepts to compare:

### Spike A — connect-by-drag

- meaningful connection points become salient when relevant;
- drag from one point to another creates an adaptive structural/mechanical link;
- reconnect/remove through the same direct object grammar;
- component follows endpoints immediately;
- optional snap and exact numeric edit remain secondary affordances.

### Spike B — place component, then adapt endpoints

- choose a recognizable component/link from a minimal palette;
- place it in space;
- snap or directly move its functional endpoints onto the construction;
- component adapts its length/orientation to the installation.

### Spike C — hybrid

- direct endpoint-to-endpoint creation for simple adaptive links;
- component placement for semantically richer parts such as damper/bearing/steering elements;
- both use the same visible connection language.

Do not infer a final component/data model from these spikes.

## 8. Experience-spike qualification threshold

Before the first Owner comparison, require only enough machine work to remove obvious confounds:

- page/build starts reliably;
- interaction does not crash;
- selection/drag/remove/reconnect are reachable in real Chromium;
- camera and viewport are usable;
- no stale delivery-surface problem;
- the spike clearly labels whether PLAY/physics is real, mocked or absent.

Do **not** require full Rep4-level causal regression for a look-and-feel-only spike.

The Owner question should be narrow and experiential:

- which grammar feels most like assembling a mechanism?
- can I form my own structural intention rather than discover the author's intended mutation?
- can I quickly undo/reconnect and try another idea?
- does the system reveal powerful mechanical ideas without forcing one recipe?

Natural STOP: first comparative Owner verdict. Do not integrate the winner into physics before that verdict.

## 9. Product principles reinforced by support research

External references are support evidence only, not architecture authority.

Useful ideas retained:

- **Shneiderman direct manipulation:** continuous representation of objects of interest, physical/direct actions, rapid incremental reversible operations with immediate visible consequence.
- **Resnick / Silverman construction kits:** low floor, wide walls, many paths, powerful ideas made salient rather than forced, iterate repeatedly.
- **Trailmakers:** simple connection semantics can create large combinatorial construction space.
- **Besiege / Scrap Mechanic / GearBlocks:** placement and explicit connection of simple functional pieces can produce rich machines; connection visibility and direct manipulation matter as much as part count.
- **Houde / Hill:** separate role, look-and-feel and implementation questions rather than demanding every prototype answer all three at once.
- **Lim / Stolterman / Tenenberg:** choose prototype scope/resolution as filters for the question; more fidelity is not automatically more knowledge.
