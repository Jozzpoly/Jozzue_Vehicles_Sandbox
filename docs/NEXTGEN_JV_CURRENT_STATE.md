## 3. E1 / V0 / R1 disposition

### E1

Portable evidence:

- authored edit → causal PLAY → exact BUILD recovery can have bounded value;
- preserving already placed intent can reduce operation burden;
- technical reachability does not establish a natural construction grammar;
- target identity, placeholder readability and task presentation can confound a representation question.

E1 did not establish a general reference/relation model, builder grammar, evaluator architecture, final renderer or asset pipeline.

### V0

V0 established a bounded physical causal chain:

`input → physical rack → fixed-length tie-rods → steering knuckles/wheels → contact → trajectory`

After presentation/input deconfounding, Owner hands-on established that the steering consequence was readable enough for the primitive carrier to be reused in another bounded research problem.

This is not a vehicle/product PASS.

### R1

R1 replaced preset-only steering geometry with independently authored LEFT/RIGHT pickup geometry and preserved the physical causal path to changed driving behavior.

Owner hands-on established bounded positive evidence that direct spatial construction can participate in repeated BUILD → DRIVE → BUILD experimentation.

R1 also exposed a real discoverability failure: automation could drag targets using privileged projected coordinates while the Owner initially could not see/acquire the baseline pickup handles hidden by wheel geometry.

Portable validation lesson:

> operation execution with privileged target knowledge is not evidence of human affordance discoverability.

Do not polish R1 into a generic builder.

## 4. What the JV_CORE donor-forensics phase established

The historical donor pass is closed at the decision-relevant level. Do not restart broad archaeology without a new concrete question.

Important positive donor capital:

- exact Blockbench/glTF assets can be useful without adopting historical rig/runtime authority;
- `Asset_Dumper.gltf` is a real reusable visual donor with authored `Part_Upper`, `Part_Stretch`, `Part_Lower` structure;
- the old telescoping-damper transform pattern contains a reusable visual adaptation idea: rigid end pieces and an extensible middle section.

Important negative evidence:

- historical visible suspension/steering geometry could be driven from different endpoints/bodies than the actual physical mechanism;
- old visible dampers could follow a different axis/topology from the physical coilover;
- old steering visual endpoints could differ from real tie-rod endpoints;
- visually plausible motion therefore cannot establish mechanical correspondence.

The donor phase exists as evidence, not as a foundation to modernize.

## 5. Rep2 Stage A and C0 results

### Stage A

A bounded suspension-link apparatus established:

`authored pivot / endpoint geometry → real arm body / hinge → wheel endpoint/path/contact`

Same-length/different-direction and one-side independence falsifiers showed that the spatial geometry mattered.

The apparatus did not author hinge-axis orientation and did not establish general suspension building.

### C0a

A direct force-path bench established that fixed physical component properties `k/c/restLength` plus different installation geometry can change real leverage/body response through exact live eyes.

This supports the product direction:

`component physical behavior + installation geometry → derived vehicle behavior`

rather than silently retuning component truth to preserve an intended wheel response.

### C0b

The external-force implementation seam revealed that evaluating state-dependent spring force only once before a multi-substep `World_Step` can reuse stale force through the internal substeps and pump energy materially in the tested bench.

That falsified one simple implementation path; it did not imply a required engine fork.

### C0c

For bounded linear `k > 0` spring / combined spring+damper, native Box3D distance spring can represent authored physical `k/c/restLength + eyes` when Hertz/damping ratio are derived internally from axial effective mass.

Fixed Hertz as component truth and generalized mechanism mass were strongly falsified for this purpose.

Pure damping `k=0,c>0` remains unresolved. Large-travel remapping policy remains unresolved.

## 6. C1 disposition — one mechanical authority, one visible causal projection

C1 is **CLOSED — BOUNDED TECHNICAL PASS**.

It established this bounded live chain:

`authority record → native Box3D spring → live body eyes → exact real Asset_Dumper donor → independent Three.js scene-graph readback`

Key accepted evidence:

- normal visual/physical correspondence error: `0 m` in accepted states;
- intentionally stale visual eye produced a measurable `0.0323160521063434 m` mismatch and the detector failed it;
- geometry mutation produced `0.0299701653420925 rad` response separation;
- authored `k=900`, `c=18`, `L0=0.5` remained invariant across the tested geometry mutation;
- native joint bodies, local frames, spring state, rest length, Hertz and damping ratio were read back from the live joint;
- the accepted authority page exposes no arbitrary-endpoint visual escape hatch;
- source/build and real Chromium gates passed locally and on Linux CI.

C1 therefore strengthens a durable product constraint:

> **One presented mechanical relation has one authority truth. Physics and visible representation are downstream projections of that same relation.**

This is not equivalent to `render mesh == solver geometry`. Fixed asset-local visual offsets/cosmetic detail may exist. What is rejected is a second live visual mechanical story that falsely explains a different hidden physical relation.

C1 did **not** establish Owner readability, product UX, vehicle handling, full suspension topology or architecture.

## 7. What is now relatively strong

Treat these as strong product constraints unless later evidence contradicts them:

### Short creative round trip

`build → run → observe → improve → get in and drive`

### Mechanical causal ownership

When JV presents a mechanism as causing behavior, the real mechanism should own the consequence.

### Causal visual correspondence

When a visible part/attachment/motion path is presented as that mechanism, it should project the same live mechanical relation rather than a parallel visual truth.

### Direct, permissive construction

Spatial mechanical geometry should be authorable directly where appropriate, with diagnosis preferred over paternalistic prevention.

### Physical component semantics before outcome-preserving retuning

For direct-construction experiments, changing installation geometry should normally be allowed to change the mechanical consequence while the component's authored physical properties remain stable. Explicit future intent locks such as “keep wheel rate” may later justify controlled retuning, but hidden compensation should not be the default.

### Real assets before placeholder gravity

Use real donor assets early enough to test actual adaptation/readability/correspondence semantics without turning placeholder geometry into an accidental parallel product system.

### Owner-readable consequence

Telemetry establishes technical separation, not human readability or feel. Owner hands-on remains required when the claim concerns those properties.

Current candidate classes include, but are not limited to:

These are candidates only. Reselection must compare information gain, product representativeness, mechanical causal consequence, adaptation/intent semantics, Owner testability, real-asset usefulness, implementation cost, confounds and prototype gravity.
