# R1 direct spatial steering pickup — Owner closure

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: **CLOSED with bounded positive causal-construction evidence; authoring/product quality remains explicitly unaccepted.**

## 1. Research question

R1 asked whether the Owner could directly alter the real left and right steering-arm pickup geometry in authored local X/Z, run/drive a vehicle whose steering consequence is owned by the physical rack/tie-rod/knuckle mechanism, return to BUILD with the exact authored state, and make further construction changes without reducing the experiment to a scalar slider disguised in 3D.

The question was deliberately bounded. R1 did not attempt to establish a final builder interaction grammar, component model, renderer, vehicle substrate, suspension model, asset pipeline or adaptation/intent-lock architecture.

## 3. Machine evidence carried into Owner evaluation

The final preflight established, within the bounded specimen:

- independent authored LEFT and RIGHT pickup local `{x,z}` state;
- genuine direction-sensitive geometry: equal arm radius with different pickup direction produced materially different deterministic physical response;
- physical rack, fixed-length physical tie-rods and steering knuckles remained steering authority;
- the analytical oracle observed rather than actuated the wheels;
- exact authored BUILD → RUN/DRIVE → BUILD recovery;
- asymmetric one-side edits without hidden symmetrization;
- fixed physical tie-rod constraints during PLAY;
- permissive continuation when the analytical oracle is outside its useful domain;
- robust pointer interruption semantics;
- a signed-angle branch-cut defect found during independent audit and repaired at the final checkpoint;
- final code check `42/42` plus build PASS;
- the applicable prior rendered browser suite remained `12/12` because the final micro-fix touched only analytical geometry and its regression test.

These are technical/research claims only. They did not establish human discoverability or product quality.

## 4. Owner hands-on evidence

The Owner performed broader hands-on testing than the short recordings alone capture. Recording length is a practical sampling limit and must not be interpreted as the full extent of Owner evaluation.

Observed and reported outcomes:

- moving the authored steering pickup points changed driving behavior in a perceptible way;
- the Owner continued experimenting after the recording and tested both sides, not only the first pickup visible in the captured sample;
- the experiment therefore received human evidence that independent spatial pickup authoring can survive through the physical steering mechanism into a noticeable driving consequence;
- the short BUILD → DRIVE → BUILD loop was usable enough to support repeated experimentation;
- very unusual/extreme authored geometry remained runnable and produced physical consequences rather than being silently clamped into a preset, which is compatible with the project's permissive-builder direction.

The Owner explicitly did **not** accept the current experience as finished or product-quality. The overall judgement was that the whole surface remains raw, incomplete, imperfect and requires substantial further work.

## 5. Confirmed readability/discoverability failure

The first Owner attempt exposed a real presentation/interaction confound before meaningful construction could even begin:

- the editable pickup markers were effectively hidden in/behind the wheel geometry in the baseline BUILD view;
- the Owner initially could not identify or acquire the editable targets;
- the first successful acquisition happened by chance by hitting the selectable point through the wheel;
- after a pickup was moved away from the wheel, the arm/tie-rod relationship became much more legible.

This is not merely an instruction failure. It demonstrates that technical reachability of an interaction does not imply human affordance discoverability.

The browser automation had privileged access to exact projected target coordinates and could therefore execute a drag even where a human could not see the target. Consequently:

> **operation execution with privileged target knowledge is not evidence that the target is visible, understandable or discoverable to the Owner.**

Future direct-manipulation validation should keep those claims separate.

## 6. R1 disposition

### Bounded positive result

R1 provides the first Owner-backed evidence in this project that:

> **directly authored spatial mechanical geometry can remain the real cause of a vehicle behavior, propagate through a physical mechanism into a perceptible driving consequence, and support repeated BUILD → DRIVE → BUILD experimentation.**

The result also weakens the specific falsifier that R1 would inevitably collapse into `steeringArmRadius` or another one-dimensional parameter disguised in 3D: the authored state contains independent left/right X/Z pickup geometry, and direction changes at equal arm radius produced different physical behavior.

### Still unresolved / not accepted

R1 does **not** establish:

- a satisfying or general builder interaction grammar;
- product-quality authoring feel;
- sufficient target/readability presentation;
- a general adaptive-component model;
- final automatic-fit versus intent-lock semantics;
- a general gizmo/snap/grid/numeric workflow;
- topology editing;
- a representative final vehicle;
- suspension/tire/contact fidelity;
- final visual-model or asset semantics;
- architecture authority for the V0/R1 renderer, physics integration, data structures or Web packaging.

The Owner's raw/incomplete judgement is important evidence and must not be smoothed into a product PASS merely because the causal experiment succeeded.

## 7. Stop condition

**Stop feature development on the R1 steering carrier.**

Do not respond to this closure by polishing pickup handles, adding generic gizmos, improving the primitive vehicle, introducing snap/numeric systems or extending the steering experiment into a builder framework. The discoverability failure is preserved as product/research evidence, not as an instruction to turn R1 into the product.

A future experiment may reuse a small lesson or repair only if its own validity requires it.

## 8. Portable lessons

Carry forward:

1. Direct spatial authoring can participate in a real mechanical causal path to driving consequence.
2. The Owner can use a short construction→driving→construction loop for genuine experimentation.
3. Permissive extreme geometry can be useful evidence and should not be automatically forbidden.
4. Technical drag reachability and human target discoverability are distinct validation questions.
5. Primitive/occluded presentation can dominate a construction experiment even when mechanics are valid.
6. A successful steering experiment is insufficient to generalize a builder architecture; the next representative problem should be materially different.
7. Real assets/visual semantics should enter before a growing placeholder system begins to define future builder assumptions.
