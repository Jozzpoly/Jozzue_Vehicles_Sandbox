## Scope

Batch 0 compared two interaction-only topology grammars on the same suspension-corner workbench:

- A — drag directly from one explicit connection point to another;
- B — choose the relation/component type, then nominate endpoint A and endpoint B.

Both allowed create/delete/undo/reconnect operations and used the real `Asset_Dumper.gltf` donor. The spike made no physical-causality claim.

## Owner result

Owner verdict: **WEAK POSITIVE / INSUFFICIENT FOR INTEGRATION**.

The Owner described the experience as only "a tiny bit better" than the preceding Rep4 apparatus. The screen recording confirms that topology agency increased — the Owner created and removed multiple relations and produced configurations not pre-authored by the experiment — but the dominant mental model remained an abstract graph of glowing sockets and links rather than handling and assembling physical vehicle components.

This is not a failure of topology agency itself. It is evidence that endpoint-graph editing alone does not provide enough experiential distance from the previous hardpoint-tuning family to justify causal-physics integration yet.

## Earned

- create/delete/reconnect/undo topology operations are operationally reachable in a lightweight browser workbench;
- direct topology editing gives some positive experiential movement beyond fixed-mechanism hardpoint tuning;
- both endpoint-first and tool-first relation creation are viable enough to remain donor interaction patterns;
- dynamic use of the real skinned damper donor is feasible after skeleton-aware cloning.

## Not earned

- neither A nor B is selected as the JV builder grammar;
- no claim that explicit sockets should be the final attachment ontology;
- no physical-causality or vehicle-behavior claim;
- no construction-feel PASS;
- no justification for polishing A/B or integrating them into the Rep4 physics carrier.

## Next falsification

Move to a materially different interaction family rather than refining A/B:

> **Family C — component-as-object / component-in-hand.**

The next spike should make the component itself the primary object of action. The Owner should take a real component from a spatial rack/work area, move it as an object, attach one end, attach the other, detach/reconnect an endpoint and remove or reposition the component. Explicit sockets may remain apparatus aids, but the interaction should test whether handling a part feels more like assembling a vehicle than authoring an abstract relation graph.

Keep the spike interaction-only until Owner evidence justifies a causal bridge.
